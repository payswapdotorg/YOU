# F1 Operator Capture — No Product-Owner Biometric Data Required

The product owner does not need to be the person captured.

## Accepted subject
Use a consenting internal tester, contractor, advisor or volunteer.

## Required consent
Capture consent must explicitly state:
- what is captured;
- why it is captured;
- which product tests will use it;
- whether the sample may be retained;
- whether it may be used for training;
- deletion/withdrawal process.

Training permission is separate and default-denied.

## Capture protocol
The Studio should guide the subject through:
1. face/front;
2. face/turn left/right;
3. upper body;
4. full body;
5. hands;
6. turn-around;
7. short walking sequence;
8. optional speech/performance sequence.

The protocol may be optimized by the Capture Scientist but the actual instructions for the acceptance fixture must be persisted with the capture.

## Evidence
Store a content-addressed evidence manifest and quality report. Record provenance, consent grant and deletion policy.

## Acceptance
F1 passes only when:
capture -> consent -> liveness -> quality -> reconstruction -> TwinVersion -> review -> deletion/export test
has real evidence.

Synthetic or public-dataset evidence may supplement the benchmark, but does not close F1.
